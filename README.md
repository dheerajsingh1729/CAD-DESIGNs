UAV Structural Design & FEA Simulation Portfolio


This repository contains a comprehensive collection of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) structural designs, focusing on multi-rotor frame optimization, assembly workflows, and finite element analysis (FEA). These projects demonstrate the transition from conceptual parametric sketches to manufacturing-ready physical prototypes.

🛠 Tech Stack
CAD Software: Autodesk Fusion 360 (Education License)

Analysis: Static Stress & Dynamic Event Simulation

Prototyping: FDM 3D Printing (PLA/Carbon Fiber Reinforced PETG)

📂 Project Highlights
1. High-Fidelity Drone Frame Assembly
Description: A complex multi-part assembly of a racing/cinematic drone frame featuring integrated motor mounts, standoffs, and protective canopies.

Key Features: * Full assembly management including fasteners (Hexagon Thin Nuts DIN 439-2).

Exploded views for assembly documentation and maintenance planning.

Component-level design of Top Plates, ESC Plates, and Arm Canopies.

2. Structural Validation & Simulation
Description: Implementation of engineering-first design principles using Fusion 360’s simulation workspace.

Capabilities:

Static Stress Analysis: Identifying high-stress concentrations on frame arms to optimize material thickness.

Dynamic Event Simulation: Modeling impact scenarios and vibration characteristics (Mesh/Results study).

3. Rapid Prototyping & Physical Verification
Description: Moving from the digital twin to physical hardware.

Workflow:

Optimization of CAD geometry for 3D printing workflows (orientation and support minimization).

Fabrication of custom drone arms and central hub plates.

🚀 Key Engineering Skills Demonstrated
Parametric Modeling: Designs are scalable based on payload and mission requirements.

Design for Manufacturing (DfM): Converting complex concepts into manufacturable 3D-printed parts.

System-Level Thinking: Integrating electronics mounts (GPS, ESC, FC) into the structural frame for a cohesive system.
